DIRECTION A · RECOMMENDED The arc and its chord A circular arc, closed by the chord that joins its two ends. Literally the definition of a chord in geometry, and a nod to Arc's legacy: Chord is what comes to close the arc. The chord takes the Space's color.
DIRECTION B The chord on the ring A thin ring, crossed by a thick chord. More abstract, closer to a classic browser icon (the globe's circle). Legible even at 16 px, but the lineage with Arc is less obvious.
DIRECTION C The segment The full circular segment, cut by its chord. Very dense, very "app icon", but it reads more like a reversed D than a C, and loses the premium finesse we aim for.
