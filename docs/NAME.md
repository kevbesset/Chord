# The name "Chord": what is free and what is taken

Checked October 27, 2026, from public sources (whois, GitHub's API, web searches of
trademark records). This is not legal advice: before an announcement or a commercial
edition, a search by a trademark attorney (or at least in the official databases, see
below) is still needed.

## Domains

| Domain | Status |
| --- | --- |
| chord.app, chord.dev, chord.so | taken |
| chordbrowser.com | taken, registered on October 22, 2026 (Cloudflare): check whether it is yours |
| chordbrowser.app, getchord.app, usechord.app, trychord.app | taken |
| chordbrowser.org | free |

The site lives at `kevbesset.github.io/Chord`, which needs no domain. A free domain to
take before announcing: `chordbrowser.org`, unless `chordbrowser.com` is already yours.

## Trademarks

- **United States (USPTO):** at least two live "CHORD" word marks for software as a
  service, in class 42: serial 88314479 (data analysis and management, likely Chord
  Commerce, chord.co) and serial 99002757 (a marketing platform for authors and
  publishers). Older "CHORD" filings for software in class 9 (serial 74698262) are
  abandoned.
- **Europe (EUIPO) and France (INPI):** not checked here: their search engines need a
  browser. To do: [EUIPO eSearch plus](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/) and
  [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) for "CHORD" in classes 9 and 42, and
  [INPI's database](https://data.inpi.fr/) for France.
- **Known users of the word:** Chord Electronics (British hi-fi maker, class 9 for its
  devices), Chord Commerce (US commerce data platform). Neither makes a browser, and
  "chord" is a common word, which narrows what a mark covers; a mark in class 9 for
  software would still be the one to watch.

## What it means

Nothing blocks the preview as it is. Before an announcement: take a domain, and check
EUIPO and INPI for "CHORD" in classes 9 and 42. Before a commercial edition (VISION.md),
an attorney's search. If a conflict shows up, the mark and the story (the chord that
closes the arc) can carry a longer name: "Chord Browser".
