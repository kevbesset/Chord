# The name "Chord": what is free and what is taken

Checked October 27 and 28, 2026, from public sources (whois, GitHub's API, TMview,
EUIPO, WIPO and INPI databases). This is not legal advice: before an announcement or a
commercial edition, a trademark attorney's search is still needed.

## Trademarks

### United States (USPTO)

At least two live "CHORD" word marks for software as a service, in class 42: serial
88314479 (data analysis and management, likely Chord Commerce, chord.co) and serial
99002757 (a marketing platform for authors and publishers). Older "CHORD" filings for
software in class 9 (serial 74698262) are abandoned.

### France (INPI)

No live French mark "CHORD": the French ones are expired or closed (CHORD 4071044,
Visconti Développement, classes 35, 41, 42; CHORD 1176217, class 9; CHORD EXPLORER
4219543). The EU marks above apply in France all the same.

## What it means

Not legal advice. Nobody holds "CHORD" for a web browser, but four holders have it for
software or electronics, in the classes a browser falls in (9, and 42 for its online
services):

- Using the name as it is today (an open source preview) carries a small risk: the
  goods differ, and coexistence is common between marks for different software.
- Registering "CHORD" for a web browser in class 9 would likely meet an opposition, from
  The Chord Company (which has opposed a class 9 software filing before) or Byte Forge.
- The risk grows with visibility and money: a commercial edition, paid services (sync,
  teams), or a large audience make a letter from a holder more likely, and a forced
  rename then costs far more than now.

Before an announcement or paid services: an attorney's search and opinion, or a name
that can be registered. If Chord stays, keep it with the mark and the full name "Chord
Browser" everywhere, which sets it apart from the holders above without making it
registrable on its own.
