# Code of Conduct

Chord is a small project, and it should be a pleasant place to take part in: in issues,
pull requests, discussions and anywhere else people talk about it in its name.

## What is expected

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including in disagreement.
- Assume good faith. Most people contribute in their free time.
- Criticize work, never people. Explain what is wrong and, when you can, how to make it
  right.
- Accept that a "no" to an idea or a pull request is about the project's direction, not
  about you.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Persistent disruption: spam, trolling, derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Kevin Besset, who maintains Chord, may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull
requests, and block anyone whose behavior goes against this code, temporarily or for
good.

To report a problem, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment in question, or
contact Kevin privately through his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/kevbesset).
Reports are kept confidential.
